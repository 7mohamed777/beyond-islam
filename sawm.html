🌙 Sawm (Fasting) in Islam - The Fourth Pillar

Sawm (fasting) during the month of Ramadan is the fourth pillar of Islam. It is a profound act of worship that combines physical discipline with spiritual elevation, teaching self-restraint, empathy for the poor, and sincere devotion to Allah. Fasting from dawn to sunset, Muslims abstain from food, drink, and marital relations while focusing on prayer, Quran, and charity.

🌙 Fourth Pillar Obligatory for all adult Muslims
🤲 Spiritual Purification Cleanses the soul and increases taqwa
❤️ Empathy & Charity Understanding hunger of the poor

📖 The Divine Command in the Quran

Fasting was prescribed for Muslims as it was for previous nations, establishing it as a universal practice of faith across all divine revelations.

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:183
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ
"O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you that you may become righteous."
Verse 183
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:185
شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِي أُنزِلَ فِيهِ الْقُرْآنُ هُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ وَبَيِّنَاتٍ مِّنَ الْهُدَىٰ وَالْفُرْقَانِ ۚ فَمَن شَهِدَ مِنكُمُ الشَّهْرَ فَلْيَصُمْهُ
"The month of Ramadan in which was revealed the Quran, a guidance for mankind and clear proofs of guidance and criterion. So whoever sights the month, let him fast it."
Verse 185
Purpose of Fasting:

The ultimate goal of fasting is to attain "taqwa" - God-consciousness, righteousness, and awareness of Allah in all aspects of life. Fasting trains the soul to prioritize divine commands over physical desires.

🌙 Ramadan: The Month of Quran and Mercy

Ramadan is the holiest month in the Islamic calendar - the month when the Quran was first revealed, when the gates of Paradise are opened, the gates of Hellfire are closed, and the devils are chained.

Prophetic Hadith
"When Ramadan begins, the gates of Paradise are opened, the gates of Hell are closed, and the devils are chained."
Bukhari & Muslim
Virtue of Ramadan
"Whoever fasts Ramadan out of faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven."
Bukhari & Muslim
📖 Quran Revealed First revelation in Laylatul Qadr
🌟 Night of Power Better than 1000 months
🤲 Forgiveness Sins forgiven with sincere fasting

⏰ A Day in Ramadan: From Dawn to Sunset

🌅 Suhoor (Pre-dawn)

Last meal before fasting begins. The Prophet (S.A.W) recommended delaying Suhoor as close to Fajr as possible and said: "Take Suhoor, for in Suhoor there is blessing."

🕐 Fajr (Dawn)

Fasting begins. From this moment until sunset, the fasting person must abstain from food, drink, and marital relations.

☀️ Daylight Hours

Time of fasting, prayer, and good deeds. Increased recitation of Quran, additional prayers, charity, and avoiding sinful speech and actions.

🌆 Maghrib (Sunset)

Iftar - Breaking the fast. Traditionally with dates and water, following the Prophet's example. The dua for breaking fast: "Dhahaba al-zama' wa abtalat al-'uruq wa thabata al-ajr insha'Allah" (The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is certain, if Allah wills).

🌃 Isha & Taraweeh

Night prayer. Special Ramadan prayers (Taraweeh) are performed in congregation, where long portions of Quran are recited throughout the month.

✅ Obligation and Exemptions

👤 Required for: Every sane, adult Muslim who is physically able
👶 Not required: Children (not yet reached puberty)
🧠 Not required: Those with mental incapacity

🩺 Legitimate Exemptions with Make-up or Fidyah

✈️ Traveler May break fast, make up later
🤒 Temporary Illness May break fast, make up later
🤰 Pregnancy May break if fasting harms mother/child, make up later
🤱 Breastfeeding May break if fasting affects milk, make up later
👵 Elderly (permanent) Feed a poor person for each missed day (fidyah)
💊 Chronic Illness Feed a poor person for each missed day (fidyah)
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:184
فَمَن كَانَ مِنكُم مَّرِيضًا أَوْ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٍ فَعِدَّةٌ مِّنْ أَيَّامٍ أُخَرَ ۚ وَعَلَى الَّذِينَ يُطِيقُونَهُ فِدْيَةٌ طَعَامُ مِسْكِينٍ
"And whoever is ill or on a journey, then an equal number of other days. And upon those who are able [but with hardship] - a ransom of feeding a poor person."
Verse 184

🚫 Actions That Invalidate the Fast

Action Status Requirement
Intentional eating/drinking Breaks fast Make up + expiation (if sexual)
Intentional vomiting Breaks fast Make up only
Menstruation/postpartum bleeding Breaks fast Make up only (cannot fast in this state)
Intentional marital relations Breaks fast Make up + severe expiation (fast 60 days or feed 60 poor)
Smoking/inhalants Breaks fast Make up only
Injections (nutritional) Breaks fast Make up only
What Does NOT Break the Fast:
  • Eating/drinking by mistake or forgetfulness
  • Unintentional vomiting
  • Taking a shower or swimming (without swallowing water)
  • Using miswak or toothbrush (without swallowing)
  • Tasting food with the tip of the tongue (without swallowing)
  • Injections (non-nutritional, like medicine)
  • Eye drops, ear drops, nose drops (unless reaching throat)
  • Kissing spouse (if self-control maintained)

💫 Beyond Hunger: The Spiritual Fast

Warning from the Prophet
"Whoever does not give up false speech and acting upon it, Allah has no need of his giving up food and drink."
Bukhari
👅 Guard the Tongue No lying, backbiting, gossip
👁️ Guard the Eyes Lower gaze from forbidden
👂 Guard the Ears Avoid listening to evil
📖 Increase Quran Recite, reflect, implement
🤲 Increase Dua Supplications readily accepted
💰 Increase Charity Feed others, give generously
The Two Joys of a Fasting Person:

The Prophet (S.A.W) said: "The fasting person has two moments of joy: one when he breaks his fast, and one when he meets his Lord." The first is the physical joy of satisfying hunger, and the second is the ultimate spiritual joy of receiving Allah's reward.

🌟 Sunnah Practices During Fasting

🌅 Delay Suhoor Eat just before Fajr
🌆 Hasten Iftar Break fast immediately at sunset
🍯 Break with Dates Odd number (1, 3, 5) with water
🤲 Dua at Iftar Supplication before breaking fast
🕌 Taraweeh Prayer Night prayers in congregation
📿 I'tikaf Seclusion in mosque last 10 days

🌟 Laylatul Qadr: Better Than 1000 Months

Surah Al-Qadr 97:1-5
إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ
"Indeed, We sent it down during the Night of Decree. And what can make you know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter. Peace it is until the emergence of dawn."
Verses 1-5
📅 Last 10 Nights Seek it in odd nights (21,23,25,27,29)
🌃 Night Worship Prayer, Quran, dua throughout night
🕊️ Angels Descend Peace until dawn
Recommended Dua for Laylatul Qadr
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
"Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul 'afwa fa'fu 'anni"
(O Allah, You are Forgiving and love forgiveness, so forgive me)

⚡ Cellular Repair Autophagy removes damaged cells
❤️ Heart Health Reduces cholesterol, blood pressure
🧠 Brain Function Increased focus and clarity

Islamic Perspective on Health:

While spiritual benefits are primary, Islam recognizes the physical benefits as additional blessings. The Prophet (S.A.W) said: "Fast, and you will be healthy." The holistic approach of Islam integrates spiritual and physical well-being.

⚠️ Common Mistakes During Ramadan

🍽️ Overeating at Iftar Eat moderately, follow Sunnah
😴 Sleeping All Day Balance rest and worship
📺 Wasting Time Avoid excessive TV/entertainment
💬 Backbiting/Gossip Guard tongue strictly
😤 Anger & Arguments "I am fasting" as response
🛍️ Excessive Shopping Focus on worship, not consumerism
Prophetic Guidance on Anger:

The Prophet (S.A.W) said: "If someone fights or argues with you, say: 'I am fasting, I am fasting.'" This reminder helps maintain the spiritual state of fasting even when provoked.

🎉 Eid al-Fitr: The Festival of Breaking Fast

🤲 Zakat al-Fitr Purification of the fast
🕌 Eid Prayer Congregational prayer in morning
❤️ Community Celebration Joy, food, family, gifts
Zakat al-Fitr
"The Messenger of Allah (S.A.W) made Zakat al-Fitr obligatory as purification for the fasting person from idle talk and obscenities, and to feed the poor." (Abu Dawud)
It is given before Eid prayer, approximately the equivalent of one meal (3 kg of staple food) per person.

🌙 Sawm: The Shield and the School of the Soul

Fasting as Protection
"Fasting is a shield (or protection) from the Fire and from committing sins." (Bukhari)
🛡️ Spiritual Shield Protects from sin and Hellfire
🏫 School of Discipline Trains soul in self-control
🤝 Community Builder Unites Muslims worldwide

Sawm is far more than abstaining from food and drink. It is a comprehensive training program for the soul - teaching self-discipline, empathy for the less fortunate, gratitude for Allah's blessings, and sincere devotion. Through fasting, Muslims worldwide unite in worship, experiencing hunger together and breaking their fast together, symbolizing the universal brotherhood of Islam. As the Prophet (S.A.W) said: "There is a gate in Paradise called Ar-Rayyan, through which those who fasted will enter on the Day of Resurrection, and no one else will enter through it." (Bukhari)
